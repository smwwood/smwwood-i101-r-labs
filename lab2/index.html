Part 4: Compare scooter companies

Our grouped-summary and graph helpers use a consistent pattern: the table, the grouping or x column, and the numeric y column.
For our helpers: data tells R which table, x tells R the variable on the x-axis, and y tells R the variable on the y-axis. We generally put the independent (causal) variable on the x-axis and the dependent (responding) variable on the y-axis.
Watch

In the code below, we'll use two functions: total_by and average_by. We wrote and loaded these functions for you. For any R aficianados, our functions are based on the dplyr function summarize. The custom functions in our lab create a common interface to R that is simpler for new users!
